Paper trading only: no real orders are ever placed. Prices, fills and fees come from Polymarket's live order books. Auto-trader rule: trade only when UP or DOWN is at least 98%, fixed ₹1,100 stake. At a 99¢ entry a win pays about ₹10 and a loss costs about ₹1,100, so the rule needs a win rate above ~99% to break even.
